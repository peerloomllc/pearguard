## What's Changed

    ### ✨ New Features

    - Allow new apps automatically: a new switch in Settings lets apps your child installs be
    allowed straight away instead of waiting for your approval. Off by default, and you are
    still told about every new app and can block it.
    - Lock for a set time: locking a device now offers 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours as well as
    "until I unlock". The device frees itself when the time is up, and both of you can see when
    that will be.
    - Unpairing no longer throws your rules away. Schedules, limits and blocked apps are kept, so
    if you pair the device again you can put them all back from the child's Advanced tab.
    - If your phone is lost for good, your child's device is no longer stuck. With your PIN they
    can start removing supervision, but it takes a full day, you are told straight away and you
    can stop it with one tap.

    ### ✨ Improvements

    - You can now see when a rule change has not reached your child's phone yet. Their card says
    it is waiting for the phone instead of looking like everything is up to date.
    - A request you have not answered within a day no longer blocks your child from asking again.
    They are told nobody answered, and you can still say yes afterwards.
    - Uses far less data. Your child's phone no longer sends app icons every time you change a
    rule, and no longer resends its entire app list each time it reconnects to you.
    - Easier on the battery when your child is offline, and their phone no longer holds on to
    stale status updates to deliver later.

    ### 🐛 Bug Fixes

    - Extra time you approve while your child's phone is offline now reaches them. It used to run                                                      
    out before it arrived, while both screens said it was approved.
    - Rule changes stick. Reconnecting could quietly put your rules back to an older version,
    re-announce apps as newly installed and leave later changes silently ignored.
    - Two changes made at the same moment no longer overwrite each other.
    - Alerts survive you being offline. If enforcement was switched off on your child's device,
    or they used the PIN while you were unreachable, you hear about it when you reconnect.
    - Usage lands on the right day when you and your child are in different time zones. Their most
    recent day could be missing from your reports entirely.
    - A second parent's PIN is accepted everywhere in the app, not only on the block screen.
